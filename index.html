01 Why should you hire Tom? +

Eight years selling for disruptors against the big names: winning over cautious enterprise buyers, turning pilots into expansions and opening markets from zero. He’s ready to do the same for your team, from 30 November.

02 How does he sell? +

With a playbook and a human touch. Tom learned structured, playbook-led enterprise selling at two enterprise SaaS companies, then brought it to Jump when he built its sales function from scratch. For him, sales is both data and people: the data shows where to play and what’s working, and the people side earns the trust that closes deals. His MBA units in Data Analytics & Modelling and Financial Management have sharpened the data side, from reading the numbers to building business cases a CFO will sign.

03 What are his best results? +

151% of target in 2023 at Exactera, and 193% in 2018 at Business Systems. Exactera’s first energy contract above $300K ARR, Jump’s first paying client at £40K ARR, and the most new client wins at Global App Testing, 42% above the peer average.

04 Can he open a market from zero? +

Yes, five times, each a first for both him and the company: Norway, Greece and Iceland as new markets, German property and renewable energy as new verticals. He’s always sold for disruptors against the big names, so building his own pipeline is how he works.

05 Has he built something from scratch? +

Yes. As Head of Sales at Jump, Tom built the sales function from nothing: CRM, pricing, process, the first client and the first two hires. Outside work, he designed and built an off- grid, solar-powered campervan and lived in it for six months.

06 What’s he like to work with? +

Direct, curious and easy to be around. Expect good questions, a clear plan, and the odd campervan story. He also loves mentoring: at Exactera, his mentoring helped two junior teammates earn promotions.
